Content of "Research", introduce HEIWA here

How should we evaluate a protein model?

  I argue models that are physically aware can either:
- Predict atom coordinates with AA sequence + force field of each AA as input.
- Predict the forces experienced by each AA if all AA identity and their positions are given as input.

Triangle.jpg

We evaluated ESM3, coordinate-informed generative model, using a force-oriented task, using a thermophilic protein classification.

We found that the contribution of H-bond to protein thermophilicity is consistently negative. This error seems to be more pronounced at the protein surface.
